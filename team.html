Sam Randall

Founder, moco

Sam Randall studied Applied Mathematics and Public Health at Johns Hopkins University, where he earned his bachelor's degree. He later pursued advanced studies in mathematics at Stanford University, completing a master's degree in Mathematical and Computational Engineering.

Over the past 6 years, Sam has built and shipped systems across a range of startups as a founding engineer, scientist, and technical lead. At Athena Security, he developed a real-time computer vision system to detect and prevent physical threats. At BluelightAI, he focused on improving machine learning model performance through automated failure mode and error analysis. At Madmen.AI, he helped build an AI-driven advertising system that generates high-performing ads based on historical data. At Prime Health Technologies, he worked on an AI-powered personalized health coach integrating nutrition, behavioral science, and health data to guide individuals toward better habits at scale.

Through this experience, Sam observed that deeply understanding and analyzing real-world data enables the design of more efficient, accurate, and performant systems. This insight led him to focus on building data-aware analysis techniques that optimize machine learning inference by aligning system architecture with the actual data distributions encountered in production.

Isabelle Girard

CEO, moco

Isabelle Girard is the CEO of moco. She holds a Master of Science in Chemistry from Chimie ParisTech and an MBA from Ecole des Ponts Business School.

Isabelle brings more than ten years of leadership experience in manufacturing at large listed companies, ExxonMobil and Arkema, where she held roles in operations, process engineering and business leadership and managed a $100M+ P&L. She has led teams, owned commercial and operational results, and driven decisions in environments where performance, reliability and safety are non-negotiable. This experience gives her a direct understanding of how industrial customers evaluate, buy and deploy new technology.

As CEO, Isabelle sets moco’s strategy and leads its commercial development. She focuses on the markets where the company’s technology creates the most value: industries where data arrives faster than fixed hardware can classify it, from real-time sensor data in manufacturing and energy to robotics and semiconductors. Her goal is to turn moco’s ability to increase throughput without compromising accuracy, worst-case latency or compute into a scalable business serving the world’s most demanding industrial operators.
